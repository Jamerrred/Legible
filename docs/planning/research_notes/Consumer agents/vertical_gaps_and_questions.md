## Patterns I noticed (inference)
- Many rules are conditional on first-time/one-time status (deep-clean first visit, vet deposit, new-client records).
- Fees are split into components with different disclosure timing (venue fee vs F&B minimum; notary fee vs travel fee; retainer set only after consult).
- Eligibility gates tied to a document the customer must hold (permit, rabies record, photo ID).
- Cancellation windows differ by booking type (24h/48h/5 days) and have emergency exemptions.
- Good agent-test requests: combine a customer fact with a gate, e.g. "15.5-year-old, permit 4 months, wants lessons".
