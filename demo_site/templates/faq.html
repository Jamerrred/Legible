Frequently Asked Questions

Here's what people ask us the most. Can't find it? Give us a call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the package and the size of your vehicle. The price board on the home page has it all. Add-ons and some fees are on top, and excessive pet hair or heavily soiled vehicles may cost extra. We confirm the final price when we see your vehicle.

What size class is my car?

There's a key at the bottom of the price board. Not sure? Just tell us what you drive when you book.

What do you work on?

Cars, SUVs, pickups, minivans and vans. Electric vehicles are fine and so are soft-top convertibles.

Do I need to give you water or power?

Usually not water. We carry our own in a 65-gallon tank, which covers up to 2 vehicles. For 3 or more vehicles we need a working spigot within 100 feet.

The Skiff doesn't need power. The Cutter, Schooner and Flagship need a grounded 120-volt outlet within 75 feet of the vehicle, or we run our generator for a $30 fee. The generator can't run in an enclosed or underground garage, so without an outlet we can't do those packages in there.

Where will you park?

Your vehicle can be in a driveway, private lot, open garage or a street space, as long as it stays legal for the whole job and there's at least 3 feet clear on both sides. We also need somewhere within 100 feet for our work van.

I live in an apartment or condo, is that OK?

Sure. You do need to get the building manager's permission for the work before you book, this goes for HOA-managed lots too. We don't contact managers, and if we get refused on the day it counts as a no-show.

What should I do before you get here?

Please take out personal belongings, child seats and cargo. An adult needs to hand over the keys at the start and stay reachable until we finish. Areas still full of belongings don't get cleaned and the price doesn't drop.

How long does it take?

Depends on the size. About 1 to 1.5 hours for the Skiff, 2 to 4 hours for the Cutter, 3 to 5 hours for the Schooner and 6 to 8 hours for the Flagship. Any job of 5 hours or more with add-ons has to start in the morning window.

What time will you get here?

We work Tuesday to Saturday and we're closed Sunday and Monday. You pick an arrival window, not an exact time. Morning is 8:00 am to 9:30 am and afternoon is 1:00 pm to 2:30 pm, Tuesday to Friday. On Saturday it's the morning window only.

Can you come today?

Normally we need at least 24 hours' notice before the start of the arrival window. For same-day you have to call us before 10:00 am. We can only do a Skiff or a Cutter, only in that day's afternoon window and only if a technician is free. There's a $40 same-day fee.

Are you open on holidays?

We close for some holidays and over the winter. If a date isn't on the booking calendar we aren't working that day.

What if it rains?

If rain is in the forecast we move exterior work and let you know the evening before, and you won't lose your deposit over it. Interior-only jobs still go ahead.

I have a dog. Can you get the hair out?

Yes! Pet hair removal is an add-on for the Cutter, Schooner or Flagship. We can't add it to a Skiff and we don't do it on its own.

Does the Cutter include shampoo?

No. The Cutter doesn't include shampoo, pet hair removal or any exterior work. You can add the upholstery and carpet shampoo to a Cutter or a Schooner, and it's already in the Flagship.

Can you get rid of a smoke smell?

Usually that's our odour treatment, for smoke, mildew or a musty car. It's an ozone treatment of either 1 hour or 2 hours and the technician chooses after a smell test on arrival. It goes on a Cutter, Schooner or Flagship, we can't do it by itself.

How long does the wax last?

The Schooner comes with a spray wax. If you want something longer we have a 6-month paint sealant, but it can only be bought together with the clay bar, on a Skiff or a Schooner. The clay bar lifts bonded grit off the paint and can be added to a Skiff or Schooner too. Both are already in the Flagship.

My headlights are foggy, can you help?

Yes, headlight restoration fixes foggy or yellowed headlights. It's priced per pair at any vehicle size and takes about 45 minutes. You can book it on its own with no other service. There's no deposit for a headlight-only visit but the usual travel fee applies.

Can you do matte paint or a wrap?

We don't clay, polish or seal matte paint, satin paint or vinyl wraps, so no Flagship, clay bar or paint sealant on those. The Skiff, Cutter and Schooner are all fine.

Do you take a deposit?

Yes, it's $50 to book any job, or $150 for the Flagship, and it counts toward your total. No deposit on a headlight-only visit.

How do I pay?

The deposit is paid by card link. We take Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. The balance is due when the job is finished, and you can pay that part in cash if you prefer.

What if I need to cancel or change the date?

Cancel at least 48 hours before the start of your arrival window and you get the full deposit back. Between 24 and 48 hours you get half back, and under 24 hours nothing back.

You can reschedule once for free with at least 24 hours' notice, by phone or text only. Any later or shorter-notice change counts as a cancellation. Please make sure we can get to the car when we arrive or you could lose your deposit.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of the job and we'll come back and redo the missed area for free. We don't do cash refunds, and this doesn't cover stains or scratches that were already there.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do! They're emailed, any whole-dollar amount from $50 to $1,000, and they never expire. You can use them on any job, including deposits and fees.

Where do you go?

Take a look at the map on the home page. We're based in San Francisco and we also travel further for a fee.
